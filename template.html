How it works

Every block gets a dollar value at the current gold price: gold recovered, minus milling, minus hauling it out. A block can only come out after the blocks above it, which holds the walls near 45°. The most profitable stable pit is a minimum cut in a flow network. This page finds it with Dinic's max-flow algorithm, written from scratch, running in your browser for all 51 prices.

Before publishing, the same solver matched exhaustive search on 20,600 small deposits, and matched Google OR-Tools to the dollar at all 51 prices on Deposit No. 0009.
